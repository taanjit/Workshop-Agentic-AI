# Acme Corp — Annual and Sick Leave Policy

## Annual Leave Entitlements
All full-time employees at Acme Corp accrue annual paid leave based on their cumulative years of service:
- Employees with less than 2 years of service receive 18 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Employees with 2 to 5 years of service receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Employees with more than 5 years of service receive 28 days of paid annual leave per year.

## Leave Rollover Rules
- A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year.
- Any carried-over days must be utilized before March 31st of the new year, or they will lapse.

## Sick Leave Guidelines
- All employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually.
- A registered medical practitioner's medical certificate (doctor's note) is mandatory for any sick leave absence exceeding 3 consecutive working days.
- Sabbatical leave is not currently offered under standard employment contracts.
