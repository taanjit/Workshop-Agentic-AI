# Acme Corp — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## Hybrid Work Entitlement
- Regular full-time employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per calendar week, subject to direct manager approval.
- Core business collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

## Working From Abroad (Workation)
- Employees in good standing may work remotely from another country for a maximum of 14 days per calendar year.
- Working from abroad requires written authorization from HR at least two weeks in advance to address tax compliance and international data residency regulations.

## Public Wi-Fi Restrictions
- When operating outside verified home offices (such as cafes, airports, or hotels), employees are strictly required to operate with VPN active at all times.
