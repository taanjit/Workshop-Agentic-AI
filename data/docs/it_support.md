# Acme Corp — IT Support & Hardware Guidelines

## Virtual Private Network (VPN) Access
- All remote and hybrid employees connecting to internal servers must connect through the Acme Secure VPN.
- Self-service password and credential resets must be performed through the official IT portal at:
  https://it.acme.example/reset
- Verification requires two-factor authentication (2FA) via the company-approved mobile authenticator application.

## Hardware & Equipment Lifecycles
- Standard employee workstation laptops are replaced on a strict 4-year lifecycle refresh.
- Additional external monitors and ergonomic accessories can be requested by opening an IT ticket.

## Security Incident & Loss Reporting
- Lost or stolen laptops and mobile devices must be reported to the IT Security Team within 24 hours of incident discovery.
- For emergency password lockouts or hardware damage, submit an IT support ticket.
